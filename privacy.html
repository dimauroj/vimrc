Privacy Policy

This application accesses the Gmail account of its single authorized user (the app owner) via the Gmail API, for the purpose of sending, reading, and labeling that user's own email from software running on the user's own machine.

Data accessed: message content, attachments, labels, and settings of the authorized account. Data is transmitted only between Google's servers and the user's own computer. No data is stored on any third-party server, sold, shared, or used for advertising. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
